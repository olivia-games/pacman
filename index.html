About the game

A three-decade-old chase that still hasn't slowed down

Pac-Man 30th Anniversary is a browser tribute to one of the most recognizable arcade games ever made. The rules haven't changed in thirty years, and that's exactly the point: steer a hungry yellow circle through a neon-blue maze, clear every pellet on the board, and stay one turn ahead of four ghosts who are learning your habits faster than you'd like.

What made the original stand out in 1980 still holds up today. There's no story to follow and no tutorial to sit through - you understand the goal within five seconds of looking at the maze, and the challenge comes entirely from timing, spatial awareness, and knowing when to turn a corner instead of running straight into a ghost's path. This version keeps that same directness while running smoothly in any modern browser, on a laptop, a tablet, or a phone in your pocket.

The four ghosts each behave a little differently once you spend a few rounds with them - one tends to cut you off at intersections, another hangs back and waits. Learning those patterns is most of the game. Power pellets sitting in the four corners of the maze flip the hunt around for a few seconds, turning the ghosts blue and vulnerable, which is usually where the real scoring happens if you're brave enough to double back for them.

Because the game runs entirely client-side, there's nothing to install and nothing to configure. Tap the Play button above, let the new tab load, press start, and you're playing.

How to Play Pac-Man 30th Anniversary

New to the maze or just need a refresher - here's everything covered from load to high score.

  1. 1

    Open the maze

    Scroll to the Play button at the top of the page and tap it to open the game in a new tab - no account or download is required.

  2. 2

    Start the run

    Once the new tab loads, click inside the game window, then press Space or tap Start to spawn Pac-Man at the bottom of the maze.

  3. 3

    Learn the controls

    Use the four arrow keys on desktop, or swipe in a direction on a touchscreen, to steer Pac-Man through the corridors.

  4. 4

    Clear every pellet

    Guide Pac-Man over every small dot in the maze without letting one of the four ghosts touch him.

  5. 5

    Use power pellets wisely

    Grab a large flashing pellet in a corner to turn the ghosts blue for a short window, then chase them down for bonus points.

  6. 6

    Chase a new high score

    Clear the board to advance a level, and keep beating your own best score - the maze resets fresh each time you start again.
